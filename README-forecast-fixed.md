`main.py` writes one line — the funniest weather forecast for the city
that isn't, Breezy Bottom — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Breezy Bottom: gusts of 12 umbrellas per hour, peaking at one airborne mailbox by noon.

Exactly 87 characters, no trailing newline.
